# Luru · Release notes

When there's a new version, the app shows you the sections newer than the one you have.

## 1.13.6
- Connected to the Butler? Photos now go to Claude first, on the Butler's credit. The key lives on the relay, never on this computer. If the credit runs dry or something hiccups, the Butler and then Gemini take over, same as before. You don't click a thing. Claude's reads get a “C” in the log.
- The desktop app also picks up 1.13.5's SHIFT fix.

## 1.13.5
- SHIFT is just a Day or Night tick. If it reads as D or N and every pass agrees, it no longer turns red just because the model muttered “not sure.” A monk who can't read a tick should go back to sweeping the temple.

## 1.13.4
- Shooting straight from the phone's camera with Take Photo could land on the computer as a blank white page. The monk was drawing before the photo had fully loaded (same fix for reading photos in the web app on an iPhone). Now each photo is drawn once, trying a few methods until one works. If none does, it isn't sent, and the phone tells you to pick it from your library.
- The phone keeps a small thumbnail of every photo it sends, so a blank one is obvious. The computer flags blank pages up top, says which one, and doesn't mistake them for repeats.
- “4 forms per photo” is on by default again.

## 1.13.3
- Web: “4 forms per photo” is now on out of the box. Lay four in a cross and shoot, nothing to tick. One form per photo still reads as one. Turn it off and it stays off.

## 1.13.2
- Web: Start never just sits there now. The reason shows up right above the button, with the fix next to it. No Butler and no key? The pairing box pops up and work begins once you’re in. Another tab hogging the job? Take over. Photos already read? Read again.

## 1.13.1
- Web, “4 forms per photo”: two or three left (or just one)? Shoot them anyway. Luru finds each sheet and cuts only where there’s something to cut. Nothing gets sliced in half.

## 1.13.0
- Web: new switch, “4 forms per photo”. Lay four forms in a cross, shoot once, and Luru cuts along the gaps and reads them one by one. Wrong cut? Drag the cross. Empty corner? Skipped. Keep them tight and fill the frame.

## 1.12.3
- Bigger batches, no traffic jam: the first read leaves as soon as its photo is ready instead of waiting on the second copy.

## 1.12.2
- Two Butlers, one queue: the desk and the handheld both read now, and whoever's free gets the next photo. Time in line doesn't count as the Butler dawdling.

## 1.12.1
- A slow Butler is a Butler. Luru now waits instead of bolting for Gemini. Only a missing, unreachable, or busy Butler gets skipped.

## 1.12.0
- Photos go to the Abbot’s own butler first: hit Connect the Butler at the bottom of the right card, say the magic word once, and every photo goes there. No Gemini quota spent. Butler out, or silent for a minute? Luru turns to Gemini by itself.
- Each photo’s line now ends with who read it: Butler or G.

## 1.11.3
- Luru speaks English now. The app, the website, the key guide and the phone page all come in English and Chinese. New installs start in English; switch in ⚙ and it remembers. Computers already running Luru stay in Chinese.
- English text gets a proper Latin font instead of borrowing the Chinese one.
