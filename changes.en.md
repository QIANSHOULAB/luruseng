# Luru · Release notes

When there's a new version, the app shows you the sections newer than the one you have.

## 1.11.3
- Luru speaks English now. The app, the website, the key guide and the phone page all come in English and Chinese. New installs start in English; switch in ⚙ and it remembers. Computers already running Luru stay in Chinese.
- English text gets a proper Latin font instead of borrowing the Chinese one.
